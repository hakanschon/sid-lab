# Changelog

## 1.5.2 — Downloadable preview

- Standalone/AU download links, complete source, checksums and installation instructions.
- Verified CI-to-release publishing with repository-scoped authentication.
- MIDI Learn tests wait for observable GUI completion instead of a fixed timer delay.
- Synthesis and MIDI processing are unchanged from 1.5.1.

## 1.5.1 — Public source preparation

- Clear standalone, GarageBand, MIDI and user-preset instructions.
- Creator credits, original dependency notices and legal resources in both bundles.
- Complete dependency-inclusive source archives, public Git export and macOS CI configuration.
- Neutral SID Lab manufacturer and bundle identity; controller-independent MIDI guidance.
- Sound parameters, factory sounds and MIDI behavior unchanged from 1.5.0.

## 1.5.0 — Eight rotary controls

- Guided eight-knob MIDI Learn, individual reassignment, channel-aware bindings, cancellation and assignment inspection.
- Controller setup stored in host state, retained while changing sounds.

## 1.4.0 — Melody voices and portable presets

- Eight additional 80s string/lead/sequencer sounds; 104 factory sounds total.
- Complete save/export/import captures including recent MIDI changes.

## 1.3.0 — 80s synth-pop

- Sixteen original Alphaville-inspired patches in a new category.

## 1.2.0 — Disco sound variation

- Independent disco timbres, filter-pluck envelope and compatibility with earlier sound formats.

## 1.1.0 — Sequencers, drums and visualizations

- Expanded factory bank, eight SID percussion kits, arp gates, pitch sweeps, Spectrum and Orbit displays.

## 1.0.0 — Initial instrument

- Native arm64 AU/standalone, libresidfp synthesis, mono/poly architectures, full chip editor, original factory sounds and seeded discovery.
